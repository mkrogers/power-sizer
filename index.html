Utility + battery case

Three noncoincident energy cases are sized and the largest governs: the outage bridge, the peak-cap window, and the repeating-day energy above the import cap taken from the load profile. If spare import cannot recharge the daily deficit, no battery size works and the case is flagged.

Islanded generation

Effective unit MW = nameplate × derating
Illustrative constant rate, not a fuel curve

Generator capacity covers full site peak, including noncritical loads, with no firm solar credit. Generator sizing uses MW (not kVA); verify alternator, power factor, transients and duty rating separately.

Solar addition (hybrid case)

The solar curve is generic and not a meteorological forecast. Use a real site-specific hourly PV model before decisions. No PV contribution is credited to firm nighttime capacity.

BESS sizing and life assumptions

Illustrative public specs, not vendor approval for site or islanding duty

Battery nameplate energy is discounted by SOC window × one-way discharge efficiency × EOL retention for bridge calculations. Verify the unit energy definition and avoid double-counting vendor usable-energy guarantees.

04 / Methodology and limitations

Know what the model does — and doesn't do.

Battery energy

Enameplate ≥ Pcritical × duration / (SOC window × ηdis × EOL capacity retention)

AC power is sized independently using specified headroom and PCS site availability. Grid-constraint and outage support are separately sized, not assumed simultaneous. Under an import cap, required storage is the largest net drawdown of the repeating 24-hour profile (sequent-peak method), recharging only from spare import.

Islanded firm generation

Ninstalled = ceil(Pfacility peak × (1 + reserve) / Punit, derated) + spare units

PV contributes zero firm capacity credit. In an N+1 case, the remaining generators must meet peak demand including the selected reserve.

Solar and dispatch

PV energy ≈ PVDC × user-entered AC daily specific yield

The model distributes that energy in a generic daylight curve, clips by inverter capacity and restricts PV usage to maintain online generator minimum loading. One-hour timesteps do not resolve fast transients.

Consciously excluded

Site weather and multi-day generation, transfer switching, fault current, power factor / kVA, harmonics, controls, protective relays, gas availability/pressure, actual fuel curves, start times, black start, grid forming, reliability probabilities, NFPA/NEC permitting, CAPEX and energy tariffs.

Primary references: Tesla Megapack public power/energy data ↗ NREL REopt methodology ↗ NREL PVWatts site-dependent solar resource ↗ Caterpillar generator sizing considerations ↗

Default 1.30 PUE, 15% reserve, 90% genset site derate, 30% generator minimum load, 4 kWh/kWdc/day solar yield, 9 MMBtu/MWh constant heat rate, 80% SOC window, 80% EOL retention, and 95% one-way efficiency are editable illustrative assumptions, not validated universal design defaults.
